Privacy Policy

Somna · Last updated August 22, 2026

Somna stores your sleep data (sleep/wake times, estimated stage breakdown) on your device. This data isn't sent to any server or shared with third parties. Somna has no backend server and no account system.

Apple Health

If Apple Health integration is on, Somna reads and writes sleep data only to compute your sleep score and keep your Health app up to date. Somna doesn't upload Health data to its own servers — it has none.

Camera & Microphone

If you set up a wake mission, one reference photo of the object you chose is stored on your device to match against at alarm time. Live camera frames used for that matching are processed in memory on-device using Apple's Vision framework and are never saved or sent anywhere. The microphone is used for snore/sound detection; recordings stay on your device.

Profile information

The age, height, weight, and sex you enter in your profile are used only to calculate your personal sleep goal, and never leave your device.
